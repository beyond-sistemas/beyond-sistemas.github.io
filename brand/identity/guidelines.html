01 · Overview

Brand meaning

The mark

The open ring is the limit. The gap is the opening. The dot is what lies beyond — vision, focus, the next step. The limit remains; the gaze goes past it.

Positioning

Beyond is a tech-forward personal / product brand built around clarity, expansion, and disciplined ambition. It speaks like Silicon Valley: simple marks, high contrast, no noise.

03 · Usage

Do / Don’t

Do

  • DoKeep clear space equal to the dot diameter around the mark.
  • DoUse black, white, or brand yellow for the symbol.
  • DoPlace the mark on paper, ink, or yellow fields.
  • DoKeep the gap and dot orientation consistent (upper-right).
  • DoPair with the Beyond wordmark in bold geometric sans.

Don’t

  • Don’tStretch, rotate, or skew the mark.
  • Don’tAdd drop shadows, glows, or gradients to the symbol.
  • Don’tRecolor with purple, neon rainbow, or off-brand hues.
  • Don’tClose the ring or remove the outward dot.
  • Don’tPlace low-contrast versions on busy photography.

Minimum size

Digital: 24px height · Print: 8mm height. Below that, use the symbol alone without wordmark.

Clear space

Reserve empty margin around the mark at least as thick as the ring stroke. Never crowd with other UI chrome.

Tagline · Medium weight · Sentence case

Body copy stays clean and direct. Prefer short sentences. Avoid decorative scripts in product UI. The handwritten Keven Miano signature may appear as a personal endorsement, never as the Beyond corporate wordmark.

06 · Buttons

Buttons

CTAs use soft rounded rectangles — not pills, not sharp squares. Geometry is pixel-perfect: fixed heights, even padding, and border-radius: 12px.

Do / Don’t

  • DoUse soft 12px rounded rectangles on every primary and secondary CTA.
  • DoKeep heights at 40 / 48 / 52px — no fractional rem heights.
  • DoInk fill for primary navigation CTAs; Yellow for submit / accent.
  • Don’tUse full pills (9999px), sharp 0–4px corners, or mixed radii in one view.
  • Don’tStretch label tracking or shrink height below 40px.

Inputs & rings
